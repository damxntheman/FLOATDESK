YOUR DESKTOP, IN VIEW

Your computer, a tap away.

This is an experimental view-only screen stream. Remote input, background reliability, and native PiP are not yet implemented or verified.

On the computer

Start a pairing session, then choose the screen you want to share. Your browser will request permission.

Pairing code—

Stop sharing turns off desktop video but keeps your phone paired. Share screen always asks for fresh browser permission. End session disconnects both devices. Capture requires HTTPS or localhost.

On the phone

Enter the pairing code from your computer. Use a trusted local network and an HTTPS connection.

If live video freezes, Refresh video asks the paired Mac to start a fresh video connection. It may briefly interrupt PiP; it does not bypass screen-sharing consent.

Not connected
